something i made in Roblox Studio for fun

now heres what the script does:
- lowers the pitch in every single sound effect in the game
- makes every BasePart change random colors, slowly getting darker until it is pitch black
- gradually changes the clocktime to night time
- unwelds and unanchors chunks of BaseParts and sets their material to CorrodedMetal
- deforms the limbs of every Humanoid and makes them change colors (however this does not get darker)
- gradually lowers the gravity of Workspace
- plays weird sounds from every Humanoid character
- creates dancing GUIs that pop up on your screen with an ImageLabel in which their image content is picked from a list of IDs and a TextLabel that displays text picked from a list of messages
- every decal and texture in the workspace is changed to an image picked from the aformentioned list of IDs
- gradually multiplies the size of every BasePart
